About the Department

The Department of English was established in 1979 under the guidance of erudite and self-effacing professors. It has been navigating towards its cherished ideals. It is one of the best and dedicated disciplines ceaselessly educating and energising students with quality content. It offers a comprehensive curriculum to equip students with a sound knowledge of literature and to strengthen their language and communication skills. The faculty constitutes a team of 9 dedicated members. The English Literary Association conducts various competitions to bring out the students' latent talents and innate skills. It also conducts seminars and encourages students to present papers on various genres of literature. The Department has a good collection of books. It is also equipped with a Language Lab and an Audio – Visual Lab to develop students' Communication Skills. The Department is committed to enhancing the linguistic competence and creative acumen of students. The Department has organised 3 International Conferences, 6 National seminars, 1 National level Workshop and 1 National level FDP in the last 5 years. The professionalism and the devotion of the faculty members have contributed more to the seminars, conferences and workshops. The number of participants has been prospectively increasing. We keep raising the bar with every conference and seminar.

Vision

To envision the department as a center for academic excellence, professional expertise and contextual research in Literature in English and fostering an understanding and acquisition of multiple literariness through learning, teaching, research and community involvement for the socially, economically and educationally backward students.

Mission

• To educate the socially, economically and educationally backward students in the region and provide them an opportunity to understand their role in the fulfillment of their duty towards the society. • To develop the learners communication skills in English. • To introduce the learners to literary scholarship and develop an aesthetic sensibility towards the various genres. • To develop among the learners an ability to analyse literary expressions. • To prepare the learners for their career enhancement and higher education.
