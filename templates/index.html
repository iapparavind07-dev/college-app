Sri S.R.N.M College, Sattur

Motto: Love, Wisdom and Service

Established in 1970, Sri S.R.N.M College is an autonomous institution affiliated to Madurai Kamaraj University, accredited with NAAC A++ grade. The college is committed to helping students from rural areas realise their talents and build capable, socially responsible careers.
